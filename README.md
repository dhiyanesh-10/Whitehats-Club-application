**Airline traveler data breach (September 2026)**

A large breach exposed around 220 million traveler records covering nine years of airline data. The leaked information reportedly included names, dates of birth, nationality, and passport or travel-document numbers, along with flight, seat and baggage details for people who travelled to, from or through Vietnam.
